Game rules

Sun 13 Dec · 10am–1pm

How it plays

  • Teams of 3, with at least 1 female. Two on court, one off.
  • Rally scoring. First to 21, win by 2.
  • Every 5 points played, counting both teams (3–2, 6–4, 8–7…), both teams rotate.
  • No switching sides after points. You stay on your side for your 5 points.
  • Each player plays 5 points on the left, then 5 on the right, then sits out for 5. On for 10, off for 5.
  • Players can start in any order, and your team can change the order every round.
  • Keep count of points played on court and rotate on every 5.
  • When your game ends, hand in or report the score to the desk.

Who's on when

Divisions and finals

  • Two divisions, A and B. You play one game against every other team in your division.
  • Ladder: most wins, then points difference, then points scored.
  • Top 6 in each division make the finals. 1st and 2nd get a bye.
  • Quarters: 3 v 6 and 4 v 5. Semis: 1 v winner of 4 v 5, 2 v winner of 3 v 6. Then the final.
  • Matching outfits encouraged. Prizes and glory to win. Christmas fun for all.

Court layout

Division A plays on courts 1–3, Division B on courts 4–6. Hand in scores at the tournament desk.
