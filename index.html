Map to the courts

Hi everyone,

Welcome back for the rematch. Last time ATP took home the bragging rights. This time we're hosting at our home courts, and Port Stephens are coming to even the score. ATP, it's time to defend the Kitchen.

Above all this is a fun, social day: great pickleball, familiar faces, new friends and plenty of laughs between our two clubs. Play hard and fight for every point, but keep it friendly and cheer each other on.

Will ATP make it two in a row, or will Port Stephens get their revenge? We'll find out on the courts.

Getting there & parking

  • The main car park is off The Ponds Boulevard, beside the playground and the Ponds FC building.
  • Street parking along Lilypad Avenue is the closest to the courts. There's more on The Ponds Boulevard.
  • The pickleball courts are a short walk past the oval, along the path at the top of the field.
  • Give yourself time to park and walk over before 8:30am.

Team & match format

All teams are mixed. Four divisions, four teams of three per club in each.

DUPR 3.5–3.99 · Courts 1–3 DUPR 3.0–3.49 · Courts 4–6 DUPR 2.5–2.99 · Courts 7–9 DUPR 2.49 & under · Courts 10–12
  • Four rounds. Your team plays three of them and rests one. Resting? Get courtside and cheer on your club.
  • Each round is three doubles games against the same team:
Game 1Players 1 & 2
Game 2Players 1 & 3
Game 3Players 2 & 3
  • Player numbers stay the same all day.
  • Every game is 12 minutes, all courts on one clock.
  • Level at time? Play one golden point to decide it.
  • All three games are played, even if one club wins the first two.

Reporting scores

  • There are no score cards. Keep track of the score as you play.
  • Report the score to the tournament desk after every game. The player sitting out the next game (one from each club) goes to the desk together and reports it while the next two get ready.
  • After Game 3, one player from each club reports that game's score.
  • Both clubs agree the score before it goes in. The desk enters it straight away.

Courts

Tap the map to open it full size.

  • Each division plays on its own three courts.
  • ATP teams B, C and D keep their own court. ATP A (resting in Round 1) takes the court of whichever ATP team is resting.
  • PSPC teams move to a new court each round.
  • Your court for every round is on My games, On court and the TV.

Results

  • Every game won is 1 point for your club, across all four divisions.
  • 144 games in total. The first club to 73 game wins takes the Kitchen.
  • If game wins finish level, the club with more total points scored wins.
  • The Draw shows the last game's scores. The standings stay under wraps until the committee reveals them. The trophy is presented at about 1:15pm.

Social afternoon

  • Mixed ATP and PSPC teams after lunch, open to everyone, including people who didn't play in the morning.
  • Playing? Put your name on the whiteboard during a break in the morning so the committee can set the format.

Testing

Simulate fills Rounds 1 and 2 (half the tournament) with random scores so you can try the Draw, Standings, Results and TV. It's turned off once any score has been entered. Reset deletes every score and puts the timer back to "Not started" (tap twice). Teams stay as they are.

Printable sheets

A4 PDFs built from the current names and courts, the same set as the Dural interclub. Print them after the teams are entered. (C) marks each team's captain.

  • Game schedule: one page per round, every court's three games in order, plus a page with all the teams.
  • Court sheets: one page per court to pin up, listing who plays there each round, game by game.
  • Final results: club totals, team and player standings, and every score.
  • Backup score cards: just in case. One page per court with a card for each round, score boxes and a winner tick box for each game. Not part of All sheets.
